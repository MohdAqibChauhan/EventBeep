1. System Architecture Overview

[Step 1: Collect From Public Job Boards] 20 Official Company Greenhouse Boards (Razorpay, Groww, Druva, PubMatic, Zscaler, MongoDB, Rubrik, etc.) + Remotive API + Jobicy API + HackerNews Jobs API + Direct Recruiter Posts │ ▼ [Step 2: Filter & Remove Duplicates (3-Step Check)] ├─► Check 1: Clean URL Match (removes tracking links pointing to the same job) ├─► Check 2: Company + Title + City Match (catches the same role reposted across boards) └─► Check 3: Similar Title Match (catches slight title variations at the same company) │ ▼ [Step 3: AI Role & City Tagging (Python + Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite)] ├─► Groups 10 jobs per request for fast, reliable batch tagging ├─► Assigns 1 of 14 Standard Job Roles & 1 of 15 Locations (14 Indian Cities + International / Global) └─► Maps local areas (like Baner, Hinjewadi, Borivali, Andheri, Koramangala, Gurugram) to their main city │ ▼ [Step 4: Daily Auto-Refresh & Live Streaming] └─► Refreshes automatically every morning at 6:00 AM IST & streams new Recruiter jobs live on scroll

2. What Broke & How We Fixed It

# What Broke Why It Happened How We Fixed It
1 Same job appeared multiple times from different feeds Aggregator feeds reposted the exact same company job with tracking links or extra words like [Hiring Urgent] and India Pvt. Ltd. Built a 3-step duplicate filter that cleans URLs, strips company suffixes, and compares title similarity — catching and removing 95 duplicate posts.
2 Neighborhood names broke city filtering Many posts listed areas like "Baner", "Hinjewadi", "Balewadi High Street", or "Koramangala" instead of "Pune" or "Bengaluru". Added Indian neighborhood mapping rules in both our city normalizer and AI prompt so local areas map cleanly to their parent city (achieving 96% City Accuracy).
3 Older Gemini model version was retired for new keys Calling gemini-2.5-flash-lite with a new AI Studio key returned a 404 Not Found error. Upgraded our Python AI worker to Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, batched 10 jobs per call, and trimmed output JSON to 98 in + 47 out tokens/job (₹8.94 standard / ₹4.47 Batch API per 1,000 jobs).
4 Vague startup job titles ("Founder's Office Intern") During our 50-job hand check, a few posts had general titles while the description asked for Product Management (PRDs/wireframes) or AI/ML skills. Updated our AI prompt to weigh the job description alongside the title, and added a Suggest Tag Edit button so students and admins can refine any tag.
5 Showing new Recruiter posts without refreshing the student's page Auto-reloading the page while a student is scrolling jumps them back to the top and ruins their search experience. Added a live background stream so when a Recruiter publishes a job, it waits quietly and slides smoothly into the student's feed as they scroll down.

3. AI Prompt Used for Role & City Tagging

You are Beep's Senior Career Taxonomy AI.
Given a batch of Indian fresher / college internship job postings, classify each job into:
1. role_tag: Choose from our 14 standard roles (Software Engineering, Frontend & Web Dev,
   Backend & Cloud/DevOps, Data Science & AI/ML, Mobile Development, Product Design (UI/UX),
   Product Management, Marketing & Growth, Business Development & Sales, Operations & Strategy,
   Content & Social Media, Finance & Analyst, HR & Talent, QA & Testing).
2. city: Normalize into 1 of 14 Indian locations (Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR,
   Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Indore, Chandigarh, Remote (India), Pan-India / Multiple).
   - Map Baner / Hinjewadi / Kharadi / Viman Nagar / Balewadi -> Pune
   - Map Bangalore / Koramangala / HSR / Whitefield -> Bengaluru
   - Map Gurgaon / Gurugram / Noida / New Delhi -> Delhi NCR
3. confidence: Match confidence score between 0.75 and 0.99.
4. reasoning: One clear sentence explaining why this role and city were chosen.
